# User Documentation
## Shree Om Jwellers — Website Guide

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** August 15, 2026

This guide has two parts: **For Customers** and **For the Shop Admin** (owner/staff).

---

## Part A — For Customers

### A.1 Browsing the Catalogue
1. Open the website on your phone.
2. From the Home page, tap a category (e.g., "Necklaces," "Rings") or scroll featured pieces.
3. Tap any ornament to open its detail page.

### A.2 Viewing an Ornament in 3D
1. On an ornament's detail page, a 3D view loads automatically at the top.
2. **Drag with one finger** to rotate the piece.
3. **Pinch with two fingers** to zoom in/out.
4. If a piece doesn't have a 3D model yet, you'll see a regular photo gallery instead — swipe to see more angles.

### A.3 Checking Today's Gold & Silver Rate
- Look at the **bottom-right corner** of any screen — you'll see two small floating bubbles.
- The gold-colored bubble shows today's **Gold rate**; the silver-colored one shows today's **Silver rate**.
- Tap a bubble to see the full rate, unit, and when it was last updated.
- These rates are set by the shop each day — always confirm final pricing in-store.

### A.4 Enquiring About a Piece
- On any ornament page, tap **"Enquire About This Piece"** to send your name, phone number, and a message — or use the one-tap **WhatsApp/Call** buttons.
- This site does **not** support online buying, cart, or delivery — all purchases are completed **in person at the store**.

### A.5 Finding the Store
- Tap **"Visit Our Store"** or the Store Info page for the address, map, hours, and contact numbers.

## Part B — For Shop Admin

### B.1 Logging In
1. Go to the admin panel (`/admin`) on your phone.
2. Enter your username and password → tap **Login**.

### B.2 Updating Today's Gold/Silver Rate
1. From the Dashboard, tap **Update Rates**.
2. Enter the new Gold rate (₹ per gram) and Silver rate (₹ per gram).
3. Tap **Save Rate**. The bubbles on the customer site update automatically — no need to touch anything else.
4. You can scroll down to see a history of past rate updates.

*Tip: Do this first thing each business day so customers always see today's rate.*

### B.3 Adding a New Ornament
1. From the Dashboard, tap **Manage Ornaments** → **Add New**.
2. Fill in: Name, Category, Purity (e.g., 22K), Approx. Weight, Description, and any making-charge note.
3. Upload one or more photos.
4. If you have a 3D model file (`.glb`) for this piece, upload it too — this enables the rotating 3D view for customers.
5. Toggle **Visible** on, then tap **Save**.

### B.4 Hiding or Removing an Ornament
- In **Manage Ornaments**, toggle **Visible** off to temporarily hide a sold/unavailable piece, or use **Delete** to remove it permanently.

### B.5 Managing Categories
- **Manage Categories** lets you add, rename, or remove categories (e.g., add a "Bridal Sets" category).

### B.6 Viewing Customer Enquiries
1. From the Dashboard, tap **Enquiries** (a badge shows unread count).
2. Tap any enquiry to see the customer's name, phone, message, and which ornament (if any) they asked about.
3. Tap **Call** to ring them directly, or mark the enquiry **Resolved** once handled.

### B.7 Logging Out
- Tap the menu → **Logout** when you're done, especially on a shared device.
